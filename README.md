# Your Personal Brand Story

I want you to design and develop a modern, premium, professional personal portfolio website for me.

Do not start by making assumptions about me, my career, my skills, my experience, or my personal brand.

Your first job is to interview me and collect the information you need. After I answer your questions, use my answers to design and build the website around my actual personality, experience, goals, skills, and work.

The final website should feel like a real personal brand website — not a generic portfolio template.
